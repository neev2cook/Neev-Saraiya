About Me

Who I Am

My name is Neev Saraiya. I'm a young engineer, builder, and relentless tinkerer who believes that the best way to understand something is to take it apart, figure out how it works, and then build a better version. I've been fascinated by machines, electronics, and code for as long as I can remember, and that curiosity has grown into a genuine passion for robotics and embedded systems.

I approach every project with the mindset of a problem-solver. Whether it's a robot that needs to navigate around obstacles, a circuit that needs to be debugged, or a piece of code that refuses to compile, I enjoy the challenge of finding elegant, practical solutions. I'm not afraid to fail, because every failure teaches me something new and brings me one step closer to a working design.

My Journey into Robotics

My journey began with simple circuits and basic programming, but it quickly evolved into a deep interest in autonomous systems. I became captivated by the idea of building machines that could sense their environment, make decisions, and act without constant human input. That led me to explore sensor integration, motor control, and the logic that ties everything together.

Over time, I've worked with a wide range of hardware — from simple microcontrollers to industrial-grade sensors — and I've learned how to bring them together into cohesive, functional systems. I've also developed a strong appreciation for clean, well-structured code and the importance of robust design in real-world applications.

What Drives Me

I'm driven by curiosity and the desire to create things that are genuinely useful. I love the moment when a project finally works — when the motors spin the right way, the sensors read correctly, and the logic holds up under pressure. That feeling of bringing an idea to life never gets old.

I'm also motivated by the constant learning that comes with this field. Every new project introduces unfamiliar components, unfamiliar problems, and unfamiliar solutions. I embrace that uncertainty because it means I'm growing.

Projects & Experience

I've built and worked on several robotics and embedded projects, including autonomous follower robots, obstacle-avoidance platforms, and sensor-driven control systems. Each project has taught me something new about hardware, software, or the delicate balance between the two. I enjoy projects that push me to integrate multiple subsystems — vision, motion, sensing, and logic — into a single, coherent machine.

I've also participated in robotics competitions and challenges, which have helped me develop my ability to work under pressure, iterate quickly, and communicate technical ideas clearly. These experiences have shaped me into a more disciplined and resourceful engineer.

How I Work

I like to start with a clear objective, sketch out the system at a high level, and then break it down into manageable pieces. I prototype early and often, testing each subsystem before integrating them. I document as I go, because I've learned that good notes save hours of confusion later. And I never stop iterating — there's always a way to make something faster, smoother, or more reliable.

Beyond Robotics

When I'm not building robots, I enjoy learning about new technologies, exploring how things work, and sharing what I've learned with others. I believe that knowledge grows when it's shared, and I'm always happy to help someone else get started on their own project.

I also enjoy the quieter side of making — the planning, the sketching, and the problem-solving that happens away from the workbench. Some of my best ideas have come while I was just thinking about a problem, not actively working on it.

Looking Forward

I'm excited about the future of robotics and embedded systems, and I plan to keep building, learning, and pushing my skills further. My goal is to create systems that are not only functional but also elegant, reliable, and genuinely helpful to the people who use them.
